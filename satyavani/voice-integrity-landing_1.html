Voice Integrity analyzes speech for synthetic, replayed and manipulated audio — before a sensitive decision depends on it.

The problem

A cloned voice sounds exactly like the real thing — to a human ear.

Synthetic speech has crossed the point where people can reliably tell it apart from a real voice. Approvals, transfers and verification calls are increasingly built on a signal that can now be faked in seconds.

How it listens

Your voice is more than sound.

Privacy

Your voice stays yours.

Recordings are processed for analysis and are not retained by default. Sensitive audio is handled with the same care as the decisions it supports.

Processed securely
Audio is handled in a controlled processing path designed around the sensitivity of voice data.
Minimal retention
Recordings are used for the analysis they were submitted for, and not kept longer than that requires.
Sensitive by design
The system is built around the assumption that every recording deserves careful handling.
